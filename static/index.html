ABOUT THE PROJECT

Public health intelligence designed for field realities

HealthSignal is a Ghana focused screening and surveillance platform developed by Ebenezer Kwaw. It combines structured field capture, transparent decision support and aggregate public health signals.

Clinical safety notice

This development version supports screening and surveillance. It does not diagnose disease or replace clinical judgement, diagnostic testing or approved Ghana Health Service protocols.
